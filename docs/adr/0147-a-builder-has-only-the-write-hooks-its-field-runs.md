# A builder has only the write hooks its field runs

An `Obj`, a `Map` and a `OneOf` write their value through their children, and the `Id` is written from `data.id` by its client-id policy and encoder, so a `deserializeUsing()` on any of them, or a `fillUsing()` on the `Id`, was accepted and then never called. The two hooks now come from the traits `DeclaresDeserializeHook` and `DeclaresFillHook`, which only the builders whose field runs them use, so declaring one of these hooks is an undefined-method error that PHPStan reports, rather than a silent no-op. A `LogicException` at declaration was rejected because it would keep the methods visible to autocompletion and static analysis while refusing them at runtime. Relations and the open members of declared meta keep both hooks unchanged.
