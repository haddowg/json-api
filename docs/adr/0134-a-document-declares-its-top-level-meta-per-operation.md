# A document declares its top-level meta per operation

A client generated from the OpenAPI document read every top-level `meta` as an open object, so an action reporting how many items it marked, or a collection reporting its unread count, was untyped. A type now declares its members per operation through `documentMeta()`, a delete's applying to its meta-only `200`, and an action's handler declares them per status through `DeclaresActionDocumentMetaInterface`, which adapters map onto its `ActionResource`, `ActionCreated` or `MetaResult` because attribute arguments cannot hold field builders. Each declaring operation answers with a document component of its own narrowing the shared one, so a type declaring nothing keeps its documents unchanged, and operation-scoping fluents on the field builders were rejected because they would leak onto attributes.
